07 Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents
want to know.

Q: What boards do you teach?

We offer expert coaching for both CBSE and Tamil Nadu State Board students from Classes 8 to 12.

Q: Where is your tuition centre located?

Ravi's Tuition Centre is located in Govindhan Street, DRO Colony Main Rd, opp. mary ann school, Alagar Nagar, K.Pudur, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625007.

Q: What is your track record and pass rate?

With 26+ years of experience since 1999, we have trained over 5000+ students with an impressive 100% board exam success rate.

Q: How do you update parents about student progress?

We provide direct WhatsApp updates to parents with detailed progress reports after every weekly assessment.

Q: What subjects do you teach?

Mathematics, Science, Social Science, English, and Tamil.

Q: Which classes do you cover?

We provide tuition for Classes 8, 9, 10, 11, and 12.

Q: How can I enroll my child?

Contact us via WhatsApp or phone, schedule a free visit, we'll conduct a basic assessment, discuss the best program, and complete enrollment.
